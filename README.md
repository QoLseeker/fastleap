# fastleap
Two M7 leap helpers. Settings: `/fastleap` (or bind a key). Targets are set per your own class; **Off** turns one off.

- **Blood Rush click:** before the boss starts, hold a Spirit Leap / Infinileap and left click. It opens the menu and picks the class you set. Once Maxor speaks, left click is vanilla again.
- **Crystal pickup:** off by default. In Maxor's stage, when the chat says *you* picked up an Energy Crystal, it switches to your leap item and leaps to the class you set.
- Click delay and cooldown are in milliseconds. The delay counts from when the leap menu opens, not from your click.
- The stage comes from boss chat lines only. If you reconnect mid-boss it starts at Blood Rush until the next boss line.
- Both features act for you, so they carry risk on Hypixel.
